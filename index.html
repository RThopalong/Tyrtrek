Our Approach

A method, not a checklist.

Every assessment is grounded in Enterprise Security Risk Management (ESRM) and Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED) — established practices for identifying where a facility is exposed and why, not just listing what's missing.

The result is a documented, professional report: what was observed, the risk it poses, and practical mitigation options — organized so that facility staff, ownership, and outside contractors can all act on it without a security background.

The same toolkit built to run these assessments is available below, for direct use by in-house staff, independent contractors, or other assessment firms.
